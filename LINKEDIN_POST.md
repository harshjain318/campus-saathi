Ever spent half a day just trying to find out how to pay your semester fees? 😅

At MANIT Bhopal, simple campus questions (library, scholarships, a lost ID card, hostel complaints) are scattered across notice boards, PDFs and portals, mostly in formal English.

So for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge, I built **Campus Saathi (कैंपस साथी)**: a voice helpdesk where you just ask out loud, in Hindi, Hinglish or English, and it answers in your language. 🎙️

How it works:
🔹 Prisma v3.0 (@Gnani.ai) understands the spoken question, even when Hindi and English are mixed
🔹 The app finds the answer from the institute's own FAQ, so it never makes up policies
🔹 Timbre v2.5 (@Gnani.ai) speaks the reply back in a natural Indian voice

A college only needs to edit one file to publish its own answers, and adding more languages is simple because Gnani's models already cover 10 Indian languages.
